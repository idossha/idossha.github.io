Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

ShiftPurse does not collect any data.

Your shifts, pay rates, tax settings and deductions are stored only on your iPhone (and in your device backups, if you use them). The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests.

The developer cannot see your information. Deleting the app deletes your data.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page before the change takes effect.
